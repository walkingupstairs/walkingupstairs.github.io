onsight: privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Deutsch

onsight is a sight-reading app for piano, made by Joshua Reer, Humboldtstr. 9, 81543 Munich, Germany, walkingupstairs.studio@gmail.com (“we”). We are responsible for this website and for emails you send us.

The short version

onsight collects no personal data. What you practise and set in the app is sent neither to us nor to anyone else. There are no accounts.

What stays on your iPad

  • Your progress and settings are stored in the app’s own storage on your iPad, and in your iPad’s backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those switched on.
  • Notes you play on a MIDI keyboard are used to grade your playing; only the scores are kept.
  • Microphone: when you clap a rhythm, onsight listens through the microphone and analyses the sound on your iPad as you go. The sound is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your iPad; existing backups keep it until they are replaced.

onsight Pro

If you buy onsight Pro, Apple handles the payment. The app asks Apple only for prices and whether Pro is active; we don’t receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security; without it the page can’t be shown. This may happen in the USA: GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss parts), for which the EU Commission has found the USA adequate. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We don’t see these logs. How long GitHub keeps them depends on that purpose; its privacy statement names no fixed period.

Emails

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they’re no longer needed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when it’s about your purchase or the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f), our interest in answering you. Writing to us is voluntary. Our mailbox is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gmail); emails may also be processed by Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the same framework.

Your rights

You may ask what data we hold about you (such as an email you sent), and have it corrected, deleted, restricted, or given to you in a portable form. You may complain to any data-protection authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us, the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

Right to object: you may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to our use of your data based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).
