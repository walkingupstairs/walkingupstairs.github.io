Connecting a keyboard

  • USB MIDI keyboard: plug it into the iPad (with an adapter if needed). onsight finds it by itself.
  • Bluetooth MIDI keyboard: in onsight, go to Settings › Input › Connect Bluetooth keyboard…, then choose your keyboard. After that it connects by itself.
  • No keyboard: turn on Settings › Input › Show on-screen keyboard, or set Assessment to Rate myself and rate each run yourself.

Clapping rhythms

If you said no when onsight asked for the microphone, turn it on in iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

onsight Pro

  • New iPad, or Pro not showing: in onsight, go to Settings › About › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Cancel a subscription: in the iPad’s Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

  • Your progress is saved on your iPad and in its backups; there is no account.
  • To start over, go to Progress › Reset progress.
