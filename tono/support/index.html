Playing your instrument (Instrument mode)

  • Silent or Instrument: Note Trainer and Interval Training have a Silent / Instrument switch on their cards. Silent needs nothing: you answer on screen. Instrument listens to the note you play.
  • “Microphone access is required”: you said no when Tono asked. Turn it on in the iPhone’s Settings › Apps › Tono › Microphone, or tap Open Settings on that screen.
  • A note isn’t recognised: play one note at a time, in a quiet room, and hold it until the ring fills. Tono hears the note as it sounds (concert pitch), and the octave counts: it must sound in the octave written on the staff.

Daily Reminder

If the reminder doesn’t arrive, check the iPhone’s Settings › Notifications › Tono, and that Daily Reminder is on in Tono’s Settings.

Ideas for new modes

On the home screen, tap Modes, then the cat. Your idea comes straight to us.

Your progress

  • Your progress and stats are saved on your iPhone and in its backups; there is no account.
  • Deleting the app deletes them from your iPhone.
