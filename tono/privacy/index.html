Tono: privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Deutsch

Tono is an app for learning to read music and train your ear, made by Joshua Reer, Humboldtstr. 9, 81543 Munich, Germany, walkingupstairs.studio@gmail.com (“we”). We are responsible for this website, for mode ideas you send from the app and for emails you send us.

The short version

Tono has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you practise and set in the app stays on your phone. The only thing the app ever sends is a mode idea, and only when you choose to send one.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress, stats and settings are stored in the app’s own storage on your phone, and in your phone’s backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those switched on.
  • Microphone: when Tono listens to your instrument or your voice, it works out the pitch on your phone as you go. The sound is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your phone; existing backups keep it until they are replaced.

Mode ideas you send us

If you use “Suggest a mode”, Tono sends us what you put in the form: your idea, a photo if you attach one, and your name and email address if you choose to give them. It also sends the app’s version and your phone’s system (iOS), so we can tell which version you mean.

  • How it travels: the app sends the form to a small service of ours run by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), which passes it on to a private Discord channel of ours (Discord Inc., USA). Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the message and to limit spam; our service doesn’t keep it. Both companies are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss parts), which the European Commission has found gives protection equivalent to EU law (Art. 45 GDPR).
  • Why, and on what basis: we use your idea only to consider it, and your name and email only as the form says: to credit you, to ask you about your idea, or to tell you when your mode ships. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in receiving and answering suggestions for the app and in keeping spam out (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for crediting you by name, your consent in giving it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by writing to us. Sending an idea is voluntary, and name and email are optional.
  • How long: we delete the message once we have dealt with the idea and no longer need it.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security; without it the page can’t be shown. This may happen in the USA: GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss parts). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We don’t see these logs. How long GitHub keeps them depends on that purpose; its privacy statement names no fixed period.

Emails

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they’re no longer needed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when it’s about the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f), our interest in answering you. Writing to us is voluntary. Our mailbox is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gmail); emails may also be processed by Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the same framework.

Your rights

You may ask what data we hold about you (such as a mode idea or an email you sent), and have it corrected, deleted, restricted, or given to you in a portable form: write to us at the address above. You may complain to any data-protection authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us, the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

Right to object: you may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to our use of your data based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).
